ClipMaker privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

ClipMaker is a private, self-hosted tool. It runs on a machine that only its owner controls and is used only by its owner. This policy explains what data the tool accesses through Google, TikTok and Instagram and what happens to it.

Data accessed through Google

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, ClipMaker asks for two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): used to upload the owner's short videos to that channel.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): used to read the channel's name and the view, like and comment counts of the uploaded videos.

Data accessed through TikTok

When the owner connects a TikTok account, ClipMaker asks for two permissions:

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): used to read the account's name and avatar, so the owner can see which account is connected.
  • Upload videos (video.upload): used to send the owner's short videos to that account as drafts, which the owner then posts in the TikTok app.

Data accessed through Instagram

When the owner connects an Instagram professional account through Facebook Login, ClipMaker asks for permission to read the account's name, to list the Facebook Pages the account is linked to, to publish Reels to the account and to read the view counts of those Reels.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish videos to the connected accounts and to show their statistics to the owner. It is not used for advertising and is not used to train any model.

Storage

The authorization tokens are stored encrypted in a database on a machine that only the owner controls: the owner's own computer or a private virtual server rented by the owner. Video statistics are stored in the same database. No one else has access to that machine.

Sharing

ClipMaker does not sell, transfer or share Google, TikTok or Instagram user data with anyone.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google), in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions, and in Instagram under Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites. Disconnecting an account inside ClipMaker deletes its stored token.

YouTube API Services

ClipMaker uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

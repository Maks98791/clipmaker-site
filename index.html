ClipMaker

ClipMaker is a private, self-hosted tool that its owner uses to prepare short vertical videos and publish them to the owner's own YouTube channels, TikTok accounts and Instagram accounts.

What it does

  • Cuts short clips from longer videos, adds captions and prepares a title, description and tags.
  • Uploads the finished videos to the owner's YouTube channels, TikTok accounts and Instagram accounts at a scheduled time.
  • Reads view, like and comment counts of the videos it uploaded.

Who can use it

The tool runs on a machine that only the owner controls and is not offered to the public. Only the owner signs in with a Google, TikTok or Instagram account.
